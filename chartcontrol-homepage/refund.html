Refund policy

Effective: 10 August 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains when a paid ChartControl plan can be refunded, how to cancel, and how a request is handled. It covers the subscription you pay to us. It does not cover money you lose trading, or fees you pay to an exchange.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the paid subscription plans (Basic, Pro, Premium and Elite) charged by ChartControl through our payment processor, Stripe. The Free plan involves no payment and therefore nothing to refund.

ChartControl is a software service. Your orders are placed on your own exchange account with the API key you grant. We never hold your funds and we never take a position. That distinction matters here: what you pay us is a subscription for the software, not a deposit, and it is separate from anything that happens on your exchange account.

2. Free plan

The Free plan carries no charge and requires no card. If you have never paid us, there is nothing to refund and no action to take. You may stop using the Service at any time.

3. Cancelling

  • You can cancel at any time from Billing in your account. Cancelling stops the next renewal.
  • You keep access to the paid features until the end of the period you have already paid for. We do not cut you off mid-period.
  • After that period the account continues on the Free plan unless you choose to subscribe again.
  • We do not treat cancelling as a refund request. If you want money back, ask under section 4.

4. Refunds

We refund on the following basis:

  • Within 14 days of your first payment on a plan, you may request a full refund of that payment. If the plan includes a trial, the 14 days run from the first payment, not from the start of the trial.
  • After that, a period that has already begun is not refundable on a pro-rata basis. Cancelling under section 3 prevents the next charge; it does not unwind the current one.
  • If we charged you in error — a duplicate charge, a renewal after you had already cancelled, or a charge for a plan you did not select — we refund it in full, whenever it is discovered.
  • If the Service was unavailable for a sustained period through our fault, we will refund or credit the affected time. Tell us what you saw and when; we check our own records.

5. Your statutory rights

This policy is in addition to the rights you have under the consumer law that applies where you live. Where that law gives you a stronger right than this policy — including a right to withdraw from a distance purchase, or a cooling-off period — that right applies and this policy does not reduce it.

Two points are worth stating plainly. First, the 14-day refund in section 4 is our own policy, and it is longer than the withdrawal period most consumer laws require. Where your local law gives you a shorter period, ours still applies; where it gives you a longer one, yours applies. Second, where the law does allow a withdrawal right to end once performance has begun, that generally requires your prior agreement and your acknowledgement that the right is lost, obtained before access starts. We ask for that separately and do not infer it from your acceptance of the Terms of service.

Access to the paid features begins when the plan you paid for becomes active. If you are unsure how your local law applies to you, ask us and we will tell you what we can. We do not ask you to give up a right you have not been told about.

6. What is not refundable

  • Exchange fees and commissions. These are charged by your exchange, not by us, and are not ours to refund.
  • Trading losses. ChartControl provides analysis tools. It does not manage money, does not guarantee an outcome, and does not reimburse losses. See the Risk disclosure.
  • Periods already used after the 14-day window, as set out in section 4.
  • Accounts suspended for misuse, where the suspension is the reason the Service ended.

A payment dispute raised with your bank or card issuer instead of with us is slower for you and starts from a false record. Please write to us first — section 7 is a short message.

7. How to request

Email support@chartcontrol.ai from the address on your account, or use Billing in your account. Include:

  • the email address on the account,
  • the invoice or payment reference, and
  • a sentence on what you would like refunded and why.

You do not need to justify a request made inside the 14-day window. We may ask one question to confirm which payment you mean.

8. Processing and payment

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Stripe. Card refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days, though the exact timing is set by your bank or card issuer, not by us. We will confirm by email when the refund has been issued. We do not refund to a different card, account or person.

9. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the version and effective date above and, for a material change, tell you by email before it applies to you. A change never applies retroactively to a purchase already made.

Questions about this policy, or a request under it: support@chartcontrol.ai.
