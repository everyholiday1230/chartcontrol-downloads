What a read actually contains

An AI trained to read charts —
not to write essays about them.

The output is not a paragraph you have to interpret. It is a set of levels drawn on the candles you are already watching, each one traceable back to the rule that produced it.

Ask in plain language
Type the question you would ask a desk. "Is this a breakout or a trap?" The answer comes back as levels on the chart, not a wall of prose.
Levels drawn on screen
Entry zone, invalidation, targets and reward-to-risk are drawn directly onto the candles — the same candles you were already reading.
Every level has a source
Each level names the rule that produced it and the candle it came from. When the AI offers its own view, it is labelled as an opinion with stated reasons — not a promise of the next move.
Contradicting evidence
Every signal ships with the case against it. A thesis you cannot falsify is not a thesis — so the read tells you what would prove it wrong.
Server-side market tools
Candles, live prices, indicator values, a market-wide scanner and risk-based position sizing are computed server-side against timestamped data — the model reads real numbers instead of guessing them.
Your own rules
Write indicators and signal rules as formulas. They run on the same engine the AI reads from, so your logic and its logic see the same market.
96-column workspace
Build the cockpit you want — charts, depth, positions, order entry, notes and the copilot in one persistent grid. Layouts are saved per account.

Straight answers

Questions traders actually ask.

Does the AI place trades for me?
No. The copilot prefills a draft order with entry, stop and reward-to-risk. You approve, edit or discard it, and nothing is sent to the exchange without your confirmation. Where automated strategies are enabled, they run only rules the account holder set and started — with a required stop-loss and a daily loss limit, stoppable at any time.
Do you hold my funds?
Never. Your account lives on the exchange. Orders are placed on your own exchange account under your own API keys. ChartControl does not custody assets and cannot withdraw.
Is this a signal service that promises returns?
No. We publish no win rate and no performance claim. The AI reads the chart, can give its own view with entry, stop and targets, marks the levels, and shows what would prove it wrong. It is analysis, not investment advice.
What can I do on the free tier?
Full AI chart reads on any listed pair, indicators and drawings, and the 96-column workspace. No card, no deposit — free points land on signup, so you reach your first read in about a minute.
Which exchanges are supported?
KuCoin and BitMart today. KuCoin carries USDT perpetual pairs with real-time WebSocket depth; BitMart adds deep alt liquidity across spot, perpetual and futures markets. Further venues are announced only once their integration passes the full test suite.
What languages does the terminal support?
Ten, with the whole product translated — not just the marketing page: English, 한국어, 日本語, 简体中文, Español, Português, Türkçe, Tiếng Việt, हिन्दी and Filipino. The AI answers in the language you write in. Legal documents are published in every one of them.
Is my chart data private?
Yes. Reads are processed with versioned prompts against your own session, and your positions and keys are never shared with other accounts or used to train models.
