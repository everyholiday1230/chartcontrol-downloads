2. What we do not collect

  • No identity documents, passports or face photos. We do not hold your funds, so we bear no identity-verification duty; collecting identity data without a legal basis to keep it would only create exposure risk for you. Identity verification is performed by the exchange.
  • No national identification numbers.
  • No bank account or card details. We do not handle deposits or withdrawals.
  • No withdrawal permission on your exchange account is requested.

3. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract: account and exchange connection data are needed to provide the Service.
  • Legitimate interests: access records are needed to block unauthorised access and protect accounts.
  • Legal obligation: trading and consent records are kept for dispute handling and statutory compliance.
  • Consent: processing beyond these purposes (for example marketing email) is done only with your separate consent, and you can use the Service without giving it.

For residents of the European Economic Area, GDPR Article 6(1)(b), (f), (c) and (a) apply respectively.

6. Retention and deletion

  • On account closure we delete account and exchange connection data without delay.
  • Data older than the periods above is deleted.
  • Where law requires retention, we store the data separately for that period and then delete it.
  • Electronic files are deleted by means that prevent recovery.

7. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Access and correct your personal data
  • Delete your exchange API keys (directly in Settings)
  • Object to or request restriction of processing
  • Close your account (withdraw consent)
  • Review your consent records
  • Receive your data in a structured format (portability)

Send requests to the contact below. We respond within 10 days. If we refuse a request, we explain why.

8. Security measures

  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes; the original is never kept.
  • Exchange API keys are stored encrypted.
  • Transport is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Administrative reads and changes are recorded with the operator and time (audit log).
  • Administrative permissions are split by duty. For example, support staff can view user information but cannot change account status.
  • Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed periodically.

No system is perfectly secure. We take reasonable measures, but it matters that you do not reuse your password on other services and do not grant withdrawal permission to your exchange API keys.

9. Cookies

We use only a session cookie to keep you signed in and a security cookie to prevent forged requests. We use no advertising, tracking or behavioural cookies, and we embed no third-party advertising scripts.

10. Children

The Service may not be used by anyone under 19, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Accounts found to belong to someone under that age are deleted without delay.

11. Contact and complaints

If you are in the EEA or the UK you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

12. Changes to this policy

Changes are published as a new version; a version already published is never edited. Material changes are announced 30 days before they take effect, in the Service or by email.

13. Model and service improvement

This section explains how we use the data the Service produces while you use it. It is written separately from the rest of this policy because it is a separate purpose with its own consent.

What this covers

DataWhat it isRetention
AI conversationsThe questions you ask the copilot and the answers it returnsUntil consent withdrawal
Trading activityOrders, fills, positions and performance on your connected exchange account, read through your own API keyUntil consent withdrawal
Chart reads and signalsThe levels, rules and evidence the Service produced for youUntil consent withdrawal
Usage and quality metricsLatency, error rates, feature usage and evaluation scoresUntil consent withdrawal

What we use it for

  • Improving the analysis — measuring which reads were accurate and adjusting the models, prompts and rules accordingly.
  • Model and feature development — building and evaluating new analysis capabilities, signal rules and workspace features.
  • Quality assurance — reproducing and fixing errors, and detecting misuse or abuse.

How it is handled

  • Aggregated and de-identified where possible. Development work uses aggregated, statistical or de-identified material rather than your identifiable conversations. Where raw material must be reviewed, access is restricted to the engineers working on that specific issue, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Never sold, never shared with other users. Your conversations, positions and results are not sold, and are not shown to any other account.
  • Never used to contact you or to market to you. This purpose is separate from marketing, which needs its own consent.
  • Not used to make decisions about you. It is not used for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Your consent, and how to withdraw it

This processing is carried out on the basis of your consent, given separately when you create your account. That means:

  • It is optional. You can use the Service fully without giving it. Declining does not restrict any feature.
  • It is specific. Agreeing to this is not a condition of using the Service, and is not bundled with accepting the Terms of service.
  • You can withdraw at any time from your account settings. Withdrawal stops future use; it does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew.
  • You can ask what is held. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of this data at any time.

If you do not give this consent, or you withdraw it, the Service still works — the only difference is that the material is excluded from the improvement purpose described above.
