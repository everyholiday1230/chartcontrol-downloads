Programme terms.

Commercial figures are set per partner and per programme. The items marked below are the ones we confirm with you before anything is signed.

Revenue share
A share of the broker rebate the exchange pays us on your audience's trades. It is never taken from their funds, and the exact figure is set per programme.
Attribution
Decided at the moment of sign-up, through your link or your code. It cannot be applied to an account afterwards.
Payout
Your share is sent directly once it clears the programme minimum — it is not credited automatically, because we hold no wallet to credit it from.
Minimum payout
Carried over until the balance reaches the threshold. The amount and currency are fixed when the programme is switched on.
Custody
Never. Orders execute on the trader's own exchange account under their own API keys — the partner and ChartControl cannot withdraw.
Claims
No win rate, no performance promise and no signal guarantees may be published under any programme. Analysis, not investment advice.
Brand use
The official wordmark and an asset pack are supplied with the programme.

Straight answers.

Does the partner ever handle client money?
No. ChartControl is non-custodial by design. Orders run on the trader's own exchange account under their own API keys — neither ChartControl nor the partner can withdraw funds.
Can I publish performance claims to promote it?
No. We publish no win rate and no performance promise, and neither may partners. The product reads the chart and can give its own view — it does not forecast the next move. Analysis, not investment advice.
Which exchanges are covered?
KuCoin and BitMart are both live and connectable today. KuCoin carries 660+ USDT-margined perpetual pairs with real-time WebSocket depth; BitMart adds deep alt liquidity across spot, perpetual and futures markets. Further venues are announced only once their integration passes the full test suite.
Is there a cost to join?
Joining is free and there is no minimum volume to start. Your audience can begin on the free tier — no card required.
