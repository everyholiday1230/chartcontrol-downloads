Options board

Eight treatments for the chart page.

Each panel below is the real effect, running live — not a mock-up image. They are built to sit behind and around a candlestick chart, so they can be dropped onto the terminal (app.html) without competing with the candles for attention. Reply with the letters you want and I will apply them.

A

Aurora mesh

Soft multi-colour light that drifts behind a frosted glass sheet. Reads as expensive and calm — the chart stays the sharpest thing on screen because the background is deliberately out of focus.

Chart pageDashboardLight mode OKHeaviest of the eight
B

Neon grid floor

A perspective grid running toward the viewer under a magenta horizon. Signals "trading terminal" instantly and gives an empty page a floor and a direction.

Chart pageHero onlyToo loud behind candlesVery cheap
C

Glass depth stack

Three translucent panes at slightly different angles. Communicates layers — the terminal above the analysis, the analysis above the market. Panels feel physical rather than flat.

Chart pagePanels & modalsLight mode OKVery cheap
D

Beam sweep

A single band of light crossing the panel on a slow loop. Used to draw the eye to one card at a time — good on a loading state, an empty chart, or the panel you want read first.

E

Scanline terminal

Faint CRT scanlines and a vignette over terminal text. Borrows the authority of a real console. Works on the connection and status panels where the product is literally talking to an exchange.

Chart page · statusConnection wizardDark mode onlyVery cheap
F

Dot matrix

A slow-drifting field of dots with a magenta glow behind it. The most restrained option here — it gives texture to large empty areas without adding a single strong shape.

Chart pageEmpty statesLight mode OKVery cheap
G

Chart pulse glow

The panel border breathes between green and magenta. On the real terminal it can be driven by the live candle — green while price holds the entry, magenta when it approaches the invalidation. The interface reacts to the market.

Chart pagePositions & alertsReactive — needs JSVery cheap
H

Starfield parallax

Three layers of stars moving at different speeds, giving genuine depth behind the chart. The cheapest convincing 3D effect available without a WebGL engine or a downloaded 3D asset.

Chart pageHeroLight mode OKVery cheap

If you want my recommendation

  • A + C — aurora behind, glass panes in front. The most "expensive" pairing and the safest for a chart, because both stay out of the candles' way.
  • B + E — grid floor and scanlines. The strongest "this is a trading terminal" read. Best on the marketing page and the connection wizard, not behind a live chart.
  • G alone — the only option that is functional, not decorative: the panel tells you the state of the trade before you read a number. Highest value per unit of visual noise.
  • F + H — texture and depth with almost no cost. Good if you would rather the terminal stay visually quiet.
