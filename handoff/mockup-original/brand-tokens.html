Brand token unification · proposal

One brand colour, applied to the product tokens.

The public site already runs on #F00FF0. The product design system (src/tokens.css) still ships a placeholder cyan as its brand primary, so the app and the site disagree. This is the patch that closes the gap — proposed, not applied to the repository.

01Why this is not just a find-and-replace

Two measurements on the exact brand colour decide how it can be used. Both were computed from the hex, not estimated.

PairingRatioAA 4.5:1Verdict
#F00FF0 as text on the dark app background5.78:1passSafe for text, icons, focus rings
#F00FF0 as text on the light app background3.05:1failMust darken on light — the patch adds a light-theme brand
White text on a #F00FF0 fill3.48:1failButtons must use dark text, or a darker magenta fill
Dark text #0C0E15 on a #F00FF0 fill5.54:1passThis is the button treatment to use

Consequence for every button

A magenta button with a white label fails AA. The patch keeps --color-brand as the fill, but the label must switch to the inverse text token. In light theme, brand-as-text is overridden to #922391 (6.41:1).

04The one thing that needs your decision

A magenta brand sits 26.7° away from the colour the product already uses for short / down (--trade-short-500, hue 355). On a chart they can read as the same family.

Chart sample — teal/magenta long-short (default), with the brand magenta as an overlay line

Three ways to resolve it — pick one before this ships

  • Keep both. 27° is distinguishable at candle width, and the AI level is a dashed line, not a candle. Lowest risk, no further change.
  • Move the AI accent to violet. The brand stays for UI chrome only, and every chart overlay uses --ai-500 (285°) instead — the two never meet.
  • Shift short/down toward red (hue 25). Maximum separation, but it changes the default long/short identity across the whole terminal.

05Two defects found while writing the patch

1 — every "info" state was silently the brand colour

--info-500: var(--brand-primary-500) couples info to the brand. With a magenta brand, an informational notice renders in the same colour family as an error. The patch decouples it to a fixed blue at hue 250.

2 — the light theme only overrode three chart tokens

Inside [data-theme="light"] only --chart-grid, --chart-axis-text and --chart-crosshair were redefined. Candle, MA and volume colours kept their dark-theme values, which are far too light for a white background. The patch adds the missing overrides, all at 4.5:1 or better.

06The patch

Delivered alongside this page as tokens-brand-chartcontrol.css. Four blocks: the replaced brand scale, the old cyan re-registered as a selectable option, the light-theme chart overrides, and the --chart-line-1..8 tokens that replace the eight hard-coded hex values in chart-indicators.js.
